This Privacy Policy explains how Simply Gallery, developed by SimplAiDev ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies only to the Simply Gallery mobile application.

Summary

Simply Gallery is a photo and video gallery that works entirely on your device. We do not operate servers, we do not have user accounts, and we never upload, copy or transmit your photos or videos anywhere. The only information that leaves your device is the data our advertising partners need in order to show ads, described below.

Photos and videos on your device

To function as a gallery, the app reads image and video files stored on your device so it can display them, group them into albums, and show details such as file name, size, date and resolution. This happens locally on your device. Photos and videos are never sent to us or to any third party.

When you edit a photo, crop it, or set it as wallpaper, the result is written back to your device storage (for example to the Pictures/GalleryApp folder). When you use the share function, Android hands the file to the app you choose; what happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Permissions we request

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) To find and display the photos and videos stored on your device. You may grant access to selected items only; the app works with partial access.
Storage write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 10 and earlier) To save edited or cropped images back to your device.
Set wallpaper (SET_WALLPAPER, SET_WALLPAPER_HINTS) To set a selected image as your home or lock screen wallpaper.
Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) To load advertisements and to detect when a connection is available. The app does not use the internet for any other purpose.

Your choices about ads

Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before advertising data is used to personalise ads, and no advertising SDK is started until you have accepted this Privacy Policy on first launch.

You can change your choice at any time from Menu → Privacy choices inside the app. If you decline personalised ads, you will still see advertisements, but they will be selected without using your advertising identifier. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's system settings.

Information we collect ourselves

None. SimplAiDev does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal information about you. We have no servers, no analytics, and no user accounts. All app settings, such as your sort order and grid size, are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

Simply Gallery is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising partners listed above, please contact us at dhiman.ad447@gmail.com.

Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To exercise rights in relation to advertising data, including access, deletion or withdrawal of consent, please contact the relevant advertising partner using the privacy policy links above. You can stop all data collection by the advertising partners by uninstalling the app.

Security

Your photos and videos stay on your device and are protected by Android's own storage and permission model. The app does not create copies of your media outside your device.
